Sadhana Bhakti

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

Sadhana Bhakti is a private digital Sadhana journal for devotees following the guidance of Srila Bhakti Vedant Sridhar Goswami Maharaj. This page explains exactly what the application stores about you, who can see it, and what control you have over it.

The short version. Your information is used only to run your Sadhana journal and to let your mentor and the sangha administrators support your practice. It is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used to track you across other websites.

What we collect

Account details

  • Your email address, used to sign in and to reset your password.
  • Your password, stored only as a secure one-way hash by our authentication provider. It cannot be read by anyone — not by administrators, and not by us.

Profile details

Whatever you enter in your profile, which may include your first and last name, spiritual name, date of birth, sex, your Gurudev's name, your Diksha stage and initiation date, your city and centre, your mobile number, and the mentor or mentors you are connected to.

Your Sadhana records

The daily entries you submit: the date, your wake-up time, whether you remembered Gurudev, attended Mangala Arati, offered bhog, engaged in Gaudiya kirtan and Tulsi seva, which scriptures you read, which Harikatha sources you heard, and your japa mala counts. If you set up an autofill template for a typical day, that is stored as well.

What we do not collect

There is no advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking pixels, and no cookies used to follow you around the web. The application does not request your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Who can see your information

  • You can see and edit all of your own profile and records.
  • Your mentor, if one is assigned to you, has read-only access to your profile and Sadhana records. Mentors can see only their own mentees and cannot change anything.
  • Sangha administrators can see devotee profiles and Sadhana records in order to run the sangha and its reports.

These limits are enforced by the database itself through row-level security rules, not only by the screens in the application. No other devotee can see your records.

Where your information is stored

  • Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database operated by Supabase.
  • The website is served by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs including IP addresses.
  • Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. No account information is sent to Google.
  • All traffic is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.

Backups

A copy of the data is exported automatically once a day and stored in a private cloud storage account controlled by the administrator, so that nothing is lost if the database fails or is deleted. Backup copies are kept for up to 180 days and then deleted. Passwords are never included in a backup.

How long we keep it

Your profile and Sadhana records are kept for as long as your account exists, because the history of your practice is the purpose of the journal. If your account is closed, your profile and all of your Sadhana records are deleted from the live database. Copies may remain in backups until those backups age out, within 180 days.

Your choices

  • You can view and correct your profile at any time from the Profile screen.
  • You can change your password, or reset it by email if you have forgotten it.
  • You can ask for your account and records to be deleted, or for a copy of your data, by contacting us at the address below.

Children

Sadhana Bhakti is intended for devotees taking part in the sangha's practice. If you are under 18, please use it with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who can see it, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Significant changes will be communicated to devotees directly.
